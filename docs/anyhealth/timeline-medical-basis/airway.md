# Airway

Code: `app/anyhealth/timeline/issues/catalog/airway.ts`, `app/anyhealth/timeline/issues/airway/*`. Checks: `app/anyhealth/timeline/check/airway.check.ts`.

Timing convention used throughout: each effect ramps in over the **8 h before** its record date (`PRE_ROLL`). The timeline shows whole days, so this makes the record date show the documented state (the X-ray, the ER visit, the test) and not the first hour of a ramp. It also matches the record: croup episodes came on at night (10pm–2am) and were seen the next day.
