# Eyes & teeth

Code: `app/anyhealth/timeline/issues/catalog/eyes-teeth.ts`, `issues/eyes/globe.ts`, `issues/teeth/eruption.ts` (`eruptionFx`), `issues/teeth/wisdom-layer.ts`. Record facts come from `app/anyhealth/health/issues.json` (cited below as "the record"). Tooth numbers are Universal: #1 upper right third molar, #3 upper right first molar, #16 upper left third molar, #17 lower left third molar, #30 lower right first molar, #31 lower right second molar, #32 lower right third molar.

Globe centres and axial length (`GLOBE` in `issues/eyes/globe.ts`) are measurements of the atlas model, not medical numbers: centre x/y is the sclera's vertex bounds centre, z is halfway between the back of the sclera and the front of the cornea, and the model's axial length is 27.2 mm. The check `globe centres match the decoded eye geometry` re-measures them.
