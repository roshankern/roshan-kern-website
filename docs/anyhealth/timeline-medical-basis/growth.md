# Growth

Reference values for boys (the subject is male). Ages are in years; `0` means birth. "Adult" means the Snyder 1977 17–19.5 y bin, which is also the normalising row (factor 1.000). The code should always use `factor(age)`, which is `ratio(age) / ratio(adult)` taken from the **same** measurement. That factor is then applied to the rig's own adult segment. Do **not** divide a ratio from this file by a rig ratio: the landmarks differ, so ratios from different definitions don't mix. Only same-measure ratios cancel the landmark offsets.

**How the segment tables were built.** Ages 2–18 use my medians of the Snyder 1977 individual male records (NIST AnthroKids `individuals.csv`, 1,978 boys; n per cell is in the detail tables). The file holds no infants. Ages 0–1 come from the Snyder 1975 male summary tables, which include infants from 0–3 months. Those means are linearly interpolated at the bin mid-ages (0–3 mo taken as 1.5 mo, 4–6 mo as 5 mo, 7–9 mo as 8 mo, and so on). A 1975 proxy measure is chained onto the 1977 joint-landmark measure at an anchor age (3 y for limb segments, 2 y for the foot and girths), so `value(age) = L77(anchor) × P75(age) / P75(anchor)`. The `0` row is the 0–3 month bin (about 1.5 months old), held flat back to birth. Segment ratios change by less than 2% over those six weeks.

Notes on the detail table:

- **Head chin→vertex for 0–1 y** = 1.50 × tragion→vertex. The 1977 ratio chin→vertex ÷ tragion→vertex rises roughly linearly from 1.52 at 2 y to 1.61 at 10 y as the face grows, and extrapolates to about 1.50 at birth. This is an extrapolation, not a measurement.
- **Vertical closure check.** head TT + neck + trunk + thigh + shank + ankle height (sphyrion/stature ≈ 0.032–0.042) sums to 0.988–1.005 of stature at every age, including the infant rows. The code's renormalisation step therefore only absorbs about 1%.
- **1977 bins:** 2 = 1.75–2.5 y, 3 = 2.5–3.5, 4 = 3.5–4.5, then ±0.5 y around each even age, and 18 = 17.0–19.5 y. Medians are of per-person ratios (differences computed within each person), from records with sex code 1. The median stature at 16.5–19 y is 176.8 cm for code 1 against 162.9 cm for code 2, which confirms 1 = male.
- **1975 tables** give means (not medians) per age band. At 3–4 y they agree with the 1977 medians to within about 1% for the overlapping measures (hand length, knee height, sitting height).
